Shakespeare Flashcards 2 adds the next 1,000 vocabulary cards in the Shakespeare Flashcards series. Study frequently used English nouns, verbs, adjectives and adverbs on iPhone and iPad. Use it on its own or as a follow-up to the first app.

Study at your own pace

  • Tap a card to reveal its definition and part of speech.
  • Sort cards into Known or Learning.
  • Swipe from left to right to undo a sorting decision.
  • Tap a deck title to browse definitions and corpus frequency counts.
  • Continue between sessions with progress saved on your device.

Offline study

All cards and definitions are included in the app. No account is required, and the app contains no advertising or analytics.

Sources

Vocabulary selection and frequencies use the Corpus of Contemporary American English, compiled by Mark Davies. Definitions use WordNet 3.0 from Princeton University. A word may appear on more than one card when it has different parts of speech.
